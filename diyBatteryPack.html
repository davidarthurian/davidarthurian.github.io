🔋 DIY Altoids Battery Pack

A simple sixth-grade electronics project that repurposed a 12V car USB charger circuit into a homemade portable USB power source inside an Altoids tin.
Early Electronics
Power Conversion
Soldering
Repurposed Hardware

Overview

This was one of my earliest hands-on electronics projects. I built a small portable battery pack using an Altoids tin as the enclosure, a 9V battery as the power source, and the circuit board from a disassembled 12V car auxiliary USB charger.

The idea was to take something that normally plugs into a car outlet, reuse its USB charging circuit, and package it into a small portable container.

Simplified diagram of the original build.

What I Learned

  • How to take apart electronics and reuse components
  • Basic power conversion concepts
  • How circuits, batteries, and outputs connect together

Reflection

This project was simple, but it sparked my interest in engineering and building real systems from scratch.
