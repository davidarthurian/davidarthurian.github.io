⭕❌ Gobblet Tic-Tac-Toe

Classic Tic-Tac-Toe with a twist — bigger pieces can gobble up smaller ones!

Mode:

How Gobblet Tic-Tac-Toe works

  • Each player has 6 pieces: 2 small, 2 medium, 2 large.
  • Blue (P1) goes first. Pink (P2) goes second.
  • Click a piece in your hand to select it, then click a board cell to place it.
  • A larger piece can gobble (cover) any smaller piece — even your own!
  • You can also move a piece already on the board — click it to pick it up, then drop it elsewhere.
  • First to get 3 in a row wins. Watch out — uncovering a piece might reveal a win for your opponent!
